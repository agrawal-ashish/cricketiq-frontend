About CricketIQ

CricketIQ is a free cricket trivia game covering every format of the sport — Test, ODI, and T20 — along with men's and women's cricket, historic records, legendary players, grounds, and rules. It's built for cricket fans who want a quick, sharp way to test how much they actually know about the game.

What's in the game

1,000 questions across more than 20 categories, from cricketing history and iconic matches to modern-day records and franchise leagues. Questions are served fresh each round, and the game keeps track of what you've already answered correctly so you don't see repeats.

Who's behind it

CricketIQ is built and maintained by an independent developer as a passion project for the cricket community. It isn't affiliated with the ICC, BCCI, or any official cricket board.
