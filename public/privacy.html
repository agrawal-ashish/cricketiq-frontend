Privacy Policy

Last updated: [31st July 2026]

CricketIQ ("we," "our," or "the app") is a cricket trivia game. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

If you sign in with Google: we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account, via Firebase Authentication (a Google service). This is used only to recognize you as a returning player.

If you don't sign in: we store a small amount of data locally in your browser (which questions you've already answered correctly) so you don't see repeats. This stays on your device and is never sent to us.

We do not collect payment information, and we do not sell any personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services We Use

  • Google Firebase — for Google Sign-In
  • Google AdSense — to display ads that support the app. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data and manage your ad preferences at adssettings.google.com, and read Google's policies at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Render and Cloudflare — infrastructure providers that host our backend and frontend respectively. Standard technical logs (like IP address and request timing) may pass through their systems as part of normal web hosting.

Cookies

This site uses cookies primarily via Google AdSense, for ad delivery and measurement. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect ad personalization, not core gameplay.

Children's Privacy

CricketIQ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You can sign out of your Google account within the app at any time. You can also clear your browser's local storage for this site to remove locally-stored gameplay data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date above.
